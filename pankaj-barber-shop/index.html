About the shop

A proper barbershop in the Mumtaz Area of Ruwi.

You’ll find us in the Mumtaz Area of Ruwi, just behind Hotel Al Burj — an easy find whether you arrive on foot, by car or by bus. We’ve built our name on the things that actually matter in a barbershop: a steady hand, a sharp eye, and a proper conversation while you wait.

Every blade, comb and clipper is cleaned and sanitised between clients. Every cut is finished properly, and every guest leaves looking like they meant to. Whether you’re after a skin fade, a classic side part, or a tidy-up before Friday prayers, we’ll get you sorted.

  • Sanitised tools, every single client
  • Walk-ins welcome — no appointment needed
  • Men and boys of all ages
  • Open daily, morning and evening sessions

Look for Plus Code JG3M+2R in Muscat if you’re navigating by map.

What we do

Services

Four core services, done properly. Not sure what you need? Just ask when you arrive and we’ll recommend the right one.

Haircut

Classic cuts, modern styles and skin fades, tailored to your face shape, hair type and how you actually wear it.

Beard trim & shape

Clipper and scissor work to give your beard clean, balanced lines — or a clean shave if you’d rather start fresh.

Kids’ haircut

Patient, friendly cuts for younger gentlemen. No fuss, no rush — they’ll walk out looking sharp too.

Styling & finish

Blow-dried and finished with the right product, so the cut still holds hours after you leave the chair.

Sanitised tools

Combs, clippers and blades cleaned between every client.
